# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse

## Introduction

One of my favorite animated movies is **Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse**. It was released in 2023.

The movie follows Miles Morales as he explores different dimensions and meets other versions of Spider-Man.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because of its:

- **Animation** – The animation is creative and visually impressive.
- **Story** – It teaches lessons about responsibility, friendship, and making choices.
- **Characters** – Each Spider-Man has a unique personality.
- **Music** – The soundtrack makes the scenes more exciting.

## My Favorite Character

**Miles Morales**

Miles is my favorite character because he is brave, determined, and willing to make his own decisions.

Overall, this is one of my favorite movies because of its animation, interesting story, and meaningful message.
